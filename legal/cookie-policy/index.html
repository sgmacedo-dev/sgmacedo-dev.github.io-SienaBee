Cookie Policy

Informational note about cookies and similar technologies on this site.

A cookie is a small file stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels.

Cookies we use

Essential. Siena Bee is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We store only your cookie choice in your browser (local storage, key sb_cookie_consent) so that we do not ask you again on every page.

Advertising (optional). The site uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set advertising cookies or use similar technologies, but only if you choose “Accept” (or enable Advertising under “Preferences”) in the cookie banner. Until then, consent for advertising and analytics storage is set to “denied” (Google Consent Mode). Affiliate links to Amazon may set cookies on Amazon’s own site after you click them.

Changing your choice

Use the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of any page to reopen the banner and change or withdraw your consent at any time. Google also lets you manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. The site’s ads.txt file is published at the root of the site.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how some features or ads are displayed.
